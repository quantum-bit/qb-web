Why Split

A small city with an unusually deep engineering bench

Split has quietly become one of the stronger software talent markets in this part of Europe, and it grew up pointed at the American market. Early US startups opened offices here, trained people to US delivery standards, and several of those companies went on to significant exits.

Quantum bit's founder ran the local branch of one of the first of them — WHERE, Inc., the Boston location-media company acquired by eBay Inc. in 2011 and folded into PayPal. That experience, and the network on both sides of the Atlantic that came with it, is what the company has been built on ever since.

Research & development

Software-defined radio for unmanned aircraft

AMOS-BL is our own R&D programme: an adaptive, modular software-defined radio designed to fly. It handles RF spectrum monitoring and signal localisation from a UAV platform, for border surveillance, personnel movement control and logistics in areas with weak or no cellular coverage.

The programme ran for 36 months with co-financing from the European Regional Development Fund, and it is where our RF, embedded and mechanical work all meet.
