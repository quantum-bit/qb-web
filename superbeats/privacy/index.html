SuperBeats Privacy Policy

26 November 2026

Your music and settings

SuperBeats works without an account. Your imported songs, drum kits, settings and saved MIDI connections are stored on your device. We do not receive your library or track your activity across apps. Deleting the app removes its local copies. You can manage device backups separately in your Apple settings.

Optional sounds

When you open SuperBeats, it may automatically check whether Singular Sound’s BeatBuddy download is available. The download starts only if you choose it. Both the check and the download send your IP address to Singular Sound’s download host, Amazon Web Services.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. SuperBeats checks your purchase status on your device to unlock or restore Pro. We do not receive your payment card details.

Beta testing and support

If you use TestFlight, Apple automatically shares crash reports and usage information with us. This can include your name and email address. We also receive any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We process reports and support messages for our legitimate interest in fixing problems and helping users. We keep our copies only as long as needed for those purposes. You can leave the beta by choosing Stop Testing in TestFlight. Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice.

Your rights and contact

For privacy questions or to request access, correction or deletion of your data, or to object to its use, contact the developer and data controller, QUANTUM BIT d.o.o., Vrlička 29, 21000 Split, Croatia. Beta testers can also email us using the contact in TestFlight under App Details. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or with Croatia’s Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
